## NZTA comparison

[NZTA’s preliminary estimate document](https://www.nzta.govt.nz/assets/About-us/docs/oia2-2024/indicative-cost-estimate.pdf) was inspected in Helium after automated downloads returned a bot-protection HTML page. Page 1 identifies the estimates as late-2023 advice, and pp.2–3 explain assumptions. They are June 2023 dollars, require further inflation for construction timing, and NZTA warns they may not represent current GPS 2024 scopes. Consequently these ranges are contextual comparisons, not replacements for current budgets.
