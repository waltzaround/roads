# Project reference review — 9 October 2026

Added 71 references across all 35 entries, prioritising the ten entries without cost data. Existing sources are retained, with tracking parameters removed. Selected status and timeline fields were corrected; original supplied policy-era costs and lengths are labelled rather than presented as current approved budgets.

## Evidence and limits

Sources are public NZTA project pages, investment-case summaries, factsheets, maps, programme updates and historical investment decisions. Some direct page requests returned HTTP 403 or 429, so those pages were reviewed through indexed NZTA page content. Search crawl dates are not publication dates. Linked publication collections may include older material and are included as routes to supporting documents; their presence does not validate every original figure. No full budget reconciliation or exhaustive live link check was performed.

“References reviewed” dates describe this source review, not an assurance that every field has been independently revalidated. No whole-corridor cost was established for the ten uncosted legacy entries. Section costs and historical funding decisions are recorded as context only, and remain excluded from totals.
